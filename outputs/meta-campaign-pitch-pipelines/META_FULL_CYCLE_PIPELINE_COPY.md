# Meta Full-Cycle Outreach Pipeline Copy

Use this language in pitch decks when explaining the three campaign-specific outreach systems.

## Fight The Power

**Slide headline:** Fight The Power: Solar savings lead to booked reviews

| Stage | Deck Copy |
|---|---|
| Ad Promise | Homeowner clicks because the electric bill feels too high. |
| Landing Capture | Page checks bill amount, roof status, ownership, and contact consent. |
| RevOps Routing | Qualified leads route to the solar preview queue with the ad hook attached. |
| Day 0 Outreach | Rep opens with bill, roof, and fit. No cold panel pitch. |
| Appointment Path | Book a solar savings review or redirect to roof-first planning. |
| Scale Loop | Scale by cost per qualified booked review, not lowest CPL. |

**Rep opener:** You asked us to check whether your bill, roof, and home make solar worth reviewing.

**Owner scorecard:** Bill-fit rate; Valid phone rate; Booked solar review CAC; Roof-first redirects

## 15+ Year Old Roof

**Slide headline:** 15+ Year Old Roof: Aging roof concern to inspection appointment

| Stage | Deck Copy |
|---|---|
| Ad Promise | Homeowner clicks because a 15+ year roof may be close to trouble. |
| Landing Capture | Page captures roof age, concern, timing, ZIP, contact, and consent. |
| RevOps Routing | Lead routes P0 to the roofing preview queue when roof age or urgency qualifies. |
| Day 0 Outreach | Rep calls around inspection first, not replacement pressure. |
| Appointment Path | Book a free inspection, then decide repair, monitor, or replace. |
| Scale Loop | Scale by qualified inspections booked and sold-job acquisition cost. |

**Rep opener:** You came through the 15+ year roof page, so I am calling to help check it before a leak makes the decision.

**Owner scorecard:** Inspection booked rate; Show rate; Sold roof CAC; Valid phone rate

## Roof Warranty

**Slide headline:** Roof Warranty: Protection-led click to warranty-backed quote

| Stage | Deck Copy |
|---|---|
| Ad Promise | Homeowner clicks because protection, trust, and warranty matter. |
| Landing Capture | Page captures roof condition, warranty interest, timing, contact, and consent. |
| RevOps Routing | Lead routes to roofing preview with warranty context before the first call. |
| Day 0 Outreach | Rep compares roof system, certified install, warranty level, and timing. |
| Appointment Path | Book a roof review and present good, better, best options clearly. |
| Scale Loop | Scale by margin-quality appointments, quote rate, and close rate by tier. |

**Rep opener:** You came through the warranty page, so I want to compare system, installation, and protection before price by itself.

**Owner scorecard:** Quote request rate; Warranty interest rate; Booked quote CAC; Close rate by tier
