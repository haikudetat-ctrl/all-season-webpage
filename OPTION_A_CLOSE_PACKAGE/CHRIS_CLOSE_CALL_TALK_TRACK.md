# Chris Close-Call Talk Track

## Goal Of The Call

Get Chris to agree that Option A is the right first move:

```text
$6,000 buildout + $12,000/month operating retainer
```

The call should feel operator-to-operator. Avoid selling "marketing services." Sell control, speed, margin protection, and a cleaner path back toward a valuable company.

## Opening

```text
Chris, the way I would frame this is simple: you do not need another disconnected marketing vendor. You need the first version of an owned revenue system that makes your spend accountable before you scale it again.
```

```text
The website is part of that, but it is not the whole offer. The real offer is the machine between demand and sold jobs: capture the lead, preserve the context, route it correctly, give the rep the right opener, and show you which sources deserve money.
```

## Confirm The Economic Pain

Ask:

```text
Is this still the right operating rule for roofing: if a $12K roof starts carrying a $3K job cost, it does not work?
```

Then:

```text
That is why I do not want to pitch you more lead volume first. Volume without control just gives you a bigger version of the same problem.
```

## Position Option A

```text
Option A is the version where we build the owned asset and stay in the seat long enough to operate it.
```

```text
The $6K gets the system built: the rebrand-ready website, the roof quote engine, the server-side lead intake, the source tracking, and the first campaign route.
```

```text
The $12K/month is not maintenance. That is us operating the revenue layer: weekly campaign decisions, quote-flow tightening, scripts, routing, landing pages, source-quality review, reporting, and the monthly decisions on what to scale, fix, or cut.
```

## Why Not Build-Only

```text
We can do build-only, but I do not think that is the best business move here.
```

```text
The value is not just launching a site. The value is the first 90 days after launch, when we learn which sources contact, which ones set, which ones demo, and which ones produce jobs at a cost that makes sense.
```

```text
If we build it and walk away, you have a better asset. If we build it and operate it, you have a cleaner revenue system.
```

## Why Not Tool Replacement First

```text
I would not start by ripping out LeadMaster, Hatch, CallTools, or whatever dialer you choose.
```

```text
You already have pieces that can work. The failure point is the handoff. High-intent leads need to come in with context, get routed to the right queue, and be handled like someone who just raised their hand, not someone on a cold list.
```

```text
Once that layer is working, we will know which tool limitations are real and which ones were process problems.
```

## Core Proof Points To Emphasize

- Chris already knows shared leads are expensive and price-competitive.
- The old in-house handling benchmark was strong: roughly 70% contact and 45% set on high-intent web leads.
- Roofing CAC has to be managed by sold job, not CPL.
- Solar lease commoditization makes trust, roof expertise, and follow-up more important.
- A rebrand-ready owned website increases enterprise value more than another rented lead source.

## The Ask

```text
My recommendation is Option A: $6K to build it, $12K/month to operate it, and we judge the first 90 days on owner-level numbers.
```

```text
If after the first 90-day cycle the system is not earning its keep, the break clause is clean: either side can end the retainer with 30 days notice.
```

```text
If it is working, then we keep tightening the machine and start putting more money behind the sources that prove they can produce profitable jobs.
```

## Close Question

```text
Are you comfortable starting with Option A and making the first 90 days about proof?
```

If yes:

```text
Great. The next step is kickoff. We will send the access checklist, lock the source map, and start the build sprint.
```

If hesitation:

```text
Makes sense. Is the concern the buildout, the monthly operating piece, or whether the first 90 days will show enough to justify it?
```

## Objection Handling

### "Why not just build the site?"

```text
We can. That is Option B. But the problem you described is not only a site problem. It is source quality, first-touch handling, and attribution. Build-only gives you the asset. Option A gives you the asset plus the operating discipline to prove what it is worth.
```

### "We already have a CRM and follow-up tools."

```text
Good. I am not trying to replace them first. I am trying to make the lead context cleaner before it hits those tools, and make the outcome reporting cleaner after the team works it.
```

### "Can you guarantee leads or sales?"

```text
No one should guarantee that honestly. Platform cost, market behavior, and rep execution are real variables. What we guarantee is the build, the routing logic, the tracking visibility, and the operating cadence. You will know what happened and what decision to make next.
```

### "Why pay monthly?"

```text
Because the first version will not be the final answer. The money is made in the weekly tightening: which ad angle works, which form step drops people, which source sets, which script gets replies, which lead source should get cut.
```

### "What happens if it does not work?"

```text
After the first 90-day operating cycle, either side can end the retainer with 30 days notice. That gives the system enough time to prove something without locking you into a long-term commitment.
```
